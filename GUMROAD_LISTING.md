## Full Product Description

### The Hook
You're staring at a market that closes in 90 seconds. The bid-ask is 45-55. The market is pricing it 50/50. But you've seen 3,000 similar markets settle at 67%. That's edge. Edge Alert finds these for you.

### What You Get

Edge Alert scans Kalshi prediction markets 24/7 using calibrated win rates from over 80,000 historical market settlements. When actual win rates exceed market-implied probability (net of fees), you get a real-time alert with:
- The edge percentage
- Expected value per contract
- Confidence tier (based on sample size)
- Market details (ticker, current bid-ask, time to close)

**Not a trading bot.** No API keys needed for alerts. No positions taken on your behalf. This is a data pipeline — you see the signal, you decide whether to act. (And if you never trade, it's still a fascinating view into market inefficiencies.)

### Markets Covered

**Crypto:**
- BTC 15-minute direction markets (final 3 minutes of each 15-min window)
- Calibrated on 19,000+ historical markets
- High-velocity, recurring opportunity

**Sports:**
- NBA, NHL, NCAAMB in-game markets (moneyline, spread, totals)

### Why This Works

1. **Data scales truth.** 80K calibration points. Not vibes, not "my edge," not a hot take. Historical win rates validated across massive sample sizes.

2. **Fee-adjusted.** Kalshi charges $0.07 × P × (1-P) per contract. We subtract this upfront. If we're saying there's edge, there's actually edge after you pay commissions.

3. **Tier filtering.** Low-sample signals get lower confidence. High-sample signals get flagged as LOCK. You see the math.

4. **Real-time + repeatable.** Crypto windows recur every 15 minutes. Sports edges show up in the final quarter. This isn't a one-off. These are recurring inefficiencies.

5. **No locked-in sizing.** We tell you the edge exists. You decide whether $1, $5, or $25 makes sense for your account. You manage risk.
