## Step 5: Understanding Your Signal Alerts

Here's what a typical signal looks like in Telegram:

```
🟢 YES KXNBAGAME-24-LAL-OKC @ 84c
Edge: +6.2% | EV: +5.2c
WR: 92.6% vs 84.0% implied
🎯 91% complete
```

Here's what each part means:

| Field | What It Means |
|-------|--------------|
| 🟢 / 🟡 / 🔴 | **Confidence level.** Green = HIGH (strongest edge, most data). Yellow = MEDIUM. Red = SPECULATIVE (less data, higher risk). |
| YES / NO | **Which side the edge is on.** "YES" means the calibration model says YES is underpriced. "NO" means NO is underpriced. |
| KXNBAGAME-24-LAL-OKC | **The Kalshi ticker.** This identifies the specific market. KX = Kalshi, then the market type, then the event. |
| @ 84c | **Current market price.** This is what the market is trading at right now, in cents. 84c means it costs 84 cents to buy a YES contract. |
| Edge: +6.2% | **The calculated edge.** This is how much the calibrated win rate exceeds the market price, after Kalshi fees. Higher = stronger signal. |
| EV: +5.2c | **Expected value per contract.** If you bought one contract, this is the average profit the model predicts, in cents. |
| WR: 92.6% vs 84.0% implied | **Win rate comparison.** The model's historical win rate at this price level (92.6%) vs what the market price implies (84.0%). The gap is your edge. |
| 🎯 91% complete | **Game progress (sports only).** How far along the game is. Higher = more predictable outcome. |
| ⏱ 3 min left | **Time remaining (crypto only).** How many minutes until the 15-minute window closes. |

**What is a Z-score?**
A Z-score measures how far a value is from the average. In Edge Alert, it measures how much the market price deviates from what our calibration data says the true probability is. A higher Z-score means a bigger gap between market price and calibrated win rate. You don't need to understand the math — the signal already accounts for it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need a Kalshi account?**
Yes, if you want to act on the signals. Edge Alert tells you where the edge is — you place your own trades on Kalshi. We don't have access to your Kalshi account. If you don't have one, sign up at [kalshi.com](https://kalshi.com).

**Q: What time zone are signals in?**
All timestamps in signals are in UTC. Signals arrive whenever markets are active — crypto runs 24/7, sports during live games.

**Q: Can I lose money trading on these signals?**
Yes. Prediction markets carry risk. Edge Alert provides data based on historical calibration — it does not guarantee any outcome. Past signal accuracy does not guarantee future results. You are responsible for all trading decisions. This is not financial advice.

**Q: What if I miss a signal?**
Check the daily digest email for a recap of yesterday's signals. Pro subscribers can also access the historical signal archive via the API.

**Q: I'm on Basic — what am I missing vs Pro?**
Basic gets crypto signals only. Pro adds sports signals (NBA, NHL, NCAAMB, MLB), lower edge thresholds (more signals surfaced), API access for raw JSON data, the historical signal archive, and priority support.

**Q: How do I contact support?**
Email support@northstaraitools.com. Pro subscribers get priority response times.
